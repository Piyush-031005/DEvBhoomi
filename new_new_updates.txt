20. My Favorite (Nobody does this)
Uttarakhand is Alive.

The website has one internal simulation.

Everything is connected by a central "life system."

If a user rings a temple bell:

Birds across the valley take flight.
Monal birds change direction.
River ripples reach downstream.
Prayer flags begin moving.
Temple lamps flicker.
Fog shifts across the mountain.
Distant echoes return after a delay.

If the user lights a diya:

Nearby darkness subtly lifts.
Reflections appear on water.
Fireflies emerge at dusk.
A temple in the distance glows slightly brighter.

No action is isolated. Every interaction changes the world.

If I had to build the world's best Uttarakhand website, I'd base it on just three pillars:
A Living World (everything reacts to everything)
A Living History (time continuously reshapes the landscape)
A Living Culture (every artifact, tradition, and district is interactive—not just displayed)

That's the difference between a beautiful website and one that people remember after they've closed the tab.
